You can remove your data from Wakeel at any time. This page explains how, including the data that came from Facebook and Instagram.

1. Remove the Facebook and Instagram connection

Choose either way. Both delete the Page and Instagram access tokens we hold, right away:

  • In Wakeel: sign in, open Company → Publishing accounts, and press "Disconnect" next to Facebook (this also removes the linked Instagram account).
  • In Facebook: open Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites), find "Wakeel", and press Remove. Facebook then notifies us and we delete the connection automatically.

2. Ask us to delete your data

If you removed Wakeel from Facebook, and your Page and Instagram data should be deleted as well, Facebook sends us the deletion request and we delete the connected accounts and their tokens immediately. You get a confirmation code and a link to a page that confirms the deletion.

To delete your whole Wakeel account and all your company's data (posts, designs, files and connected accounts), email us from your account's email address at {{ support_email }} with the subject "Data deletion". We delete it within 30 days and confirm with you, except for anything the law requires us to keep.

3. What is deleted

  • The Facebook Page ID and name, the Instagram account ID and username, and the access tokens.
  • The Meta user ID we used to recognise your deletion request.

Posts, designs and plans you created in Wakeel stay in your company's workspace until you delete them or your account.
