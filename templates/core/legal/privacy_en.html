Wakeel ("we") is a service{% if legal_entity %} provided by {{ legal_entity }}{% endif %} that helps businesses plan, write, design and publish their social media content. This policy explains what data we collect when you use Wakeel at {{ site_url }}, how we use and protect it, and how to delete it.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: your name, email address, password (stored hashed; we can't read it) and interface language.
  • Company data: its name, business, audience and tone, its visual identity (colors, fonts and logo), links to its website and social accounts, contact numbers, and the images and files you upload. If you enter the company's website, we read its public pages to suggest these details to you.
  • Content: the content plans, posts, designs, comments and review notes created in Wakeel.
  • Data from accounts you connect:
    • Facebook and Instagram (through Meta): the Page ID and name, the Instagram professional account ID and username, and the Page access token.
    • TikTok: the account ID (open_id) and display name, and the access and refresh tokens.
    We don't access your messages, followers or passwords on these platforms, and we read nothing from them beyond the above.
  • Technical data: the cookies needed to keep you signed in and remember your language, your browser notification subscription if you turn it on, and server logs for security and troubleshooting.

2. How we use data

  • To provide the service: preparing content plans, writing posts and designing images in your brand's identity.
  • To publish to connected accounts: only posts your team approves, when you press "Publish now" or at their scheduled time if you turn on automatic posting. On TikTok we send the design to the app's inbox, and nothing is posted until you press "Post" yourself.
  • To send notifications and emails about your account.
  • To provide support and protect the service from abuse.

We don't sell your data, we don't use it for advertising, and we use Meta and TikTok data for nothing other than the publishing you ask for.

3. Who we share data with

  • AI providers (such as DeepSeek and Anthropic): we send them your company's brand profile and the content request so they can write the text. We never send them passwords or access tokens.
  • Meta and TikTok: only the posts you ask us to publish, through their official APIs.
  • Hosting and email providers we run the service on.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

4. Connected accounts and revoking access

You can disconnect any account at any time from the "Publishing accounts" page in Wakeel, and we delete its access token immediately. You can also revoke Wakeel's access in your Facebook settings (Business integrations) or your TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions).

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account and company data for as long as your account exists. Temporary images we prepare for publishing are deleted as soon as the platform has received them.

To delete your account and all your company's data (posts, designs, files and connected accounts), email us from your account's email address at {{ support_email }} with the subject "Data deletion". We delete it within 30 days and confirm with you, except for anything the law requires us to keep.

6. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), store passwords hashed, and limit each company's data to its own team members according to their roles. No method of protection is perfect, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

7. Children

Wakeel is a service for businesses and is not directed at anyone under 18.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we update the "Last updated" date above, and we tell you in Wakeel or by email if the change is significant.
