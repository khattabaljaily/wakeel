About Wakeel

An AI marketing manager working for your company

Wakeel does a marketing agency's whole job: it understands your brand, plans the month's content, writes posts in your audience's language, designs them in your identity, then publishes them on time. You just review and approve.

What does Wakeel do?

Understands your brand

It reads your company's website to suggest its profile: business, audience, tone and colors. You fill in the rest.

Plans your month

A monthly strategy with clear goals and content pillars, aware of local occasions, from Ramadan and Eid to seasons and national days.

Writes like your audience

Posts in Standard Arabic or the local dialect, with catchy headlines, clear calls to action, fitting hashtags, and short scripts for videos.

Designs in your identity

Ready-to-post designs in your colors, fonts and logo, sized right for each platform, and editable in a click.

Review and approval

One calendar for your team, with roles and permissions, and a review link your client can approve the plan from without an account.

Publishes on time

It publishes approved posts to Facebook and Instagram automatically, sends TikTok designs to the app's inbox, and prepares next month's plan on its own.

How does it work?

  1. 1
    Add your company

    Enter its website, and Wakeel suggests the brand profile for you to review.

  2. 2
    Ask for the month's plan

    Pick the platforms, how many posts and any special brief, and Wakeel prepares the plan with all its posts and designs in minutes.

  3. 3
    Review and approve

    Change anything in a click, or ask Wakeel to rewrite a post, then approve.

  4. 4
    Connect your accounts and let it post

    A step-by-step wizard connects Facebook, Instagram and TikTok, with no technical know-how needed.
