About Wakeel

A smart platform for all your company's work

Wakeel brings together what your company needs every day: social media marketing, keeping and issuing documents, AI services and team work. Its assistant, Marid, ties them together: it suggests and does what you ask, with your approval.

What does Wakeel do?

Plans and writes your marketing

A monthly strategy and posts in your audience's language, with hashtags and video scripts, aware of local occasions.

Designs in your identity and publishes

Designs in your colours, fonts and logo at each platform's size, then publishes what is approved to Facebook and Instagram on time.

Keeps your documents safe

Licences, contracts and letters, encrypted and sorted, with alerts before they expire and protected sharing links.

Issues your official documents

Writes the document with templates designed for your company on its official letterhead, and issues it as PDF or Word with a QR code anyone can verify.

Translates, summarises, extracts

Smart services for documents in 16 languages: translation, summaries, data extraction, conversion to Word and reading text from images.

Organises your team and mail

Tasks, contacts, notes, internal announcements, forms and surveys, and the company mailbox, with clear roles and permissions.

How does it work?

  1. 1
    Add your company

    Give its website and Wakeel suggests the company profile and identity for you to review.

  2. 2
    Ask for what you need

    A content plan, a document, a translation or a design. Ask from the right page, or in one sentence to Marid.

  3. 3
    Check the price, then approve

    Every service shows its itemised price before it runs, is charged from your wallet exactly as shown, and is given back if it does not complete.

  4. 4
    Review the result

    Review, edit and approve the posts and documents, then they are published, issued or shared.

Security and privacy

Your files are encrypted

Every private file of your company is stored encrypted with a key of its own, and only your team members see it, by role.

A full activity log

Important actions (opening a document, issuing, sharing, inviting a member) are recorded for the owner and administrators to review.

No price surprises

The price shown is always the price charged, and spending guards keep any unexpected use in check.
