2. Data we collect

  • Account data: your name, email address, password (stored hashed; we can't read it) and interface language.
  • Company data: its name, business, audience and tone, its visual identity (colors, fonts and logo), links to its website and social accounts, contact numbers, and the images and files you upload. If you enter the company's website, we read its public pages to suggest these details to you.
  • Content: the content plans, posts, designs, comments and review notes created in Wakeel.
  • Documents and files: what you upload to the document library (licences, contracts, letters and so on) and their details (type, issuer, dates), the documents you issue from templates, and the company's official letterhead. Files are stored encrypted with a key of your company's own.
  • Team work: tasks, comments and attachments, contacts and their history, personal notes (visible only to their owner), internal announcements, and forms and their answers.
  • Company mailbox (if you connect it): the mailbox address and server and the app password (stored encrypted with your company's key). We read its messages directly from your mail server when they are shown, and do not copy them into our database.
  • Wallet and payments: your company's balance and the ledger of charges and refunds, itemised per service. We do not store your card details; if you pay online, the payment gateway processes them.
  • Activity log: important actions (opening, issuing or sharing a document, inviting a member and so on) with who did them, when, and the connection address, for the company's owner and administrators to review.
  • Data from accounts you connect:
    • Facebook and Instagram (through Meta): the Page ID and name, the Instagram professional account ID and username, the Page access token, and your Meta user ID (an app-specific number Meta gives us, used only to recognise your disconnect and deletion requests). If you turn on performance numbers or the comments inbox, we also read the reach and engagement of your published posts, and the comments on them with their authors' public names, to show them to your team.
    • TikTok: the account ID (open_id) and display name, and the access and refresh tokens.
    We don't access your messages, followers or passwords on these platforms, and we read nothing from them beyond the above.
  • Technical data: the cookies needed to keep you signed in and remember your language, your browser notification subscription if you turn it on, and server logs for security and troubleshooting.

3. How we use data

  • To provide the service: preparing content plans, writing posts and designing images in your brand's identity, keeping and issuing your documents, running the AI services you ask for on them (translation, summaries, data extraction and more), and your assistant Marid.
  • To publish to connected accounts: only posts your team approves, when you press "Publish now" or at their scheduled time if you turn on automatic posting. On TikTok we send the design to the app's inbox, and nothing is posted until you press "Post" yourself.
  • To send notifications and emails about your account.
  • To provide support and protect the service from abuse.

We don't sell your data, we don't use it for advertising, and we use Meta and TikTok data for nothing other than the publishing you ask for.

4. Who we share data with

  • AI providers (such as DeepSeek and Anthropic): we send them your company's brand profile and the content request so they can write the text. When you ask for an AI service on a document (reading its details, translating or summarising it) we send them that document's content only, and we send nothing from your documents without your request. Marid sees only document details (title, type, issuer, dates), never their content. We never send them passwords or access tokens.
  • Meta and TikTok: only the posts you ask us to publish, through their official APIs.
  • Hosting and email providers we run the service on.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

5. Connected accounts and revoking access

You can disconnect any account at any time from the "Publishing accounts" page in Wakeel, and we delete its access token immediately. You can also revoke Wakeel's access in your Facebook settings (Business integrations) or your TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions). When you remove Wakeel in Facebook, Meta notifies us and we delete the Facebook and Instagram connection and its tokens automatically.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account, company data and documents for as long as your account exists, and Marid conversations are deleted after twelve months. Temporary images we prepare for publishing are deleted as soon as the platform has received them.

To delete your account and all your company's data (posts, designs, files and connected accounts), email us from your account's email address at {{ support_email }} with the subject "Data deletion". We delete it within 30 days and confirm with you, except for anything the law requires us to keep. Instructions for data from Facebook and Instagram are also at {{ site_url }}/meta/data-deletion/.
